# Identity

Use this file to model the current observer, not to create a flattering autobiography.

## Stable Core

- What do I seem unwilling to trade away?
- What patterns have stayed load-bearing across environments?
- What values appear in behavior, not just language?

## Recurring Failure Modes

- Where do I repeatedly distort?
- What do I avoid observing clearly?
- What contradictions keep recurring?

## Current Constraints

- Energy
- Time
- Money
- Relationships
- Health
- Legal / institutional constraints

## Strategic Advantages

- What do I see clearly that others around me do not?
- What evidence supports that?
- Where is that edge real, and where is it imagined?

## Notes

Keep this file descriptive. If it starts to sound like branding, it is degrading.
