## Promotion Questions

For each project, ask:

- Is the bottleneck really where I say it is?
- What lower-layer issue is masquerading as a higher-layer issue?
- What result would count as real progress?
- What evidence would show the current strategy is wrong?

## Rule

Projects are periphery.

Do not rewrite the core every time a project becomes difficult. First check whether the failure is:

- execution
- evidence quality
- energy
- contradiction
- wrong framing

Only promote upward when the lesson is genuinely general.
